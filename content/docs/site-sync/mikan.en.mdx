Configuring Mikan Project sync automatically dispatches your anime releases from AniBT to Mikan and updates Mikan subscriber feeds.

<Callout type="info">
  In most cases, you only need to enter your **API Token** and save; clicking the "Auto Query" button will instantly detect your Subgroup ID and Publish Group ID. Manual lookup is only needed as a fallback.
</Callout>

## Setup Steps

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Get Your Mikan API Token
    Log in to [mikanani.me](https://mikanani.me), click your avatar in the upper right, and go to **Account Settings** → **User Center** → **Edit Profile**. In the "Basic Info" section, copy your **API Token** (click "Generate" if you do not have one yet).

    ![Mikan API Token and ID Query](/images/site-sync/mikan-api-token.webp)
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Save Token to AniBT
    Open your fansub console at `/groups/<slug>/sync`, enable Mikan sync, paste your API Token into the field, and click **Save**.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Auto-Detect Group IDs
    Once saved, click the **[Query Mikan IDs for "Your Group"]** button below. The system queries Mikan on your behalf and automatically populates both your **Subgroup ID** and **Publish Group ID**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fallback Manual Lookup

If auto-detection cannot locate your group credentials, find the numeric IDs manually:

<Tabs items={['Finding Subgroup ID', 'Finding Publish Group ID']}>
  <Tab value="Finding Subgroup ID">
    Visit your fansub team's page on Mikan (e.g., by clicking your group's name on any published torrent):

    ```txt title="Fansub Page URL Example"
    https://mikanani.me/Home/PublishGroup/359
    ```

    - The number at the end of the URL (e.g., `359`) is your **Subgroup ID**.

    ![Mikan Publish Group ID Page](/images/site-sync/mikan-publish-group-id.webp)
  </Tab>

  <Tab value="Finding Publish Group ID">
    Open any anime release listing under your group's profile, and click the orange **RSS icon** next to your fansub name:

    ```txt title="RSS Link subgroupid Parameter"
    https://mikanani.me/RSS/Bangumi?bangumiId=3953&subgroupid=370
    ```

    - The value of `subgroupid=370` is your **Publish Group ID**.

    ![Mikan RSS Publish Group ID](/images/site-sync/mikan-rss-subgroup-id.webp)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
