---
title: DMHY Sync
description: Learn how to configure publisher credentials, bind your fansub group name, and test connections for DMHY synchronization.
---

Configuring DMHY sync automatically submits your anime releases from AniBT to the DMHY platform in the background.

<Callout type="warn">
  The DMHY account used must already have release privileges. The fansub group name must exactly match the team registered to that account in the DMHY backend, otherwise submissions will be rejected.
</Callout>

## Setup Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open DMHY Sync Settings
    Navigate to your fansub console at `/groups/<slug>/sync`, find the **DMHY Sync** card, toggle it on, and click **Edit Credentials**.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Enter Credentials & Group Name
    Enter your DMHY publisher username, password, and the exact fansub group name registered on DMHY.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Save and Test Connection
    Click **Save**, then click **Test Connection**. This test verifies login credentials and fansub group assignment without posting any placeholder torrents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Field Descriptions

<TypeTable
  type={{
    'Username': {
