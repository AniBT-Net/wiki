---
title: DMHY Sync
description: Enter your DMHY username, password, and subtitle group name, then save and test the connection.
---

DMHY sync requires a username, password, and the subtitle group name you use on DMHY.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Edit credentials

    In AniBT sync settings, click **Edit credentials**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter username and password

    Enter the username and password for your DMHY account.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter subtitle group name

    Enter the subtitle group name you use on DMHY.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save

    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Test connection

    Click **Test connection** and confirm it succeeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
