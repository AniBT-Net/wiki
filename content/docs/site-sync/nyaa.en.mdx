Configuring Nyaa direct sync automatically submits your anime releases from AniBT to Nyaa using your group's own account credentials.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Security Recommendation**: Please register a dedicated bot account on Nyaa for AniBT sync rather than reusing personal account passwords.
</Callout>

## Direct Sync vs. Platform Proxy

Before configuring, determine which method fits your team:

- **Direct Sync (This Page)**: Uses your team's own Nyaa username and password. No whitelist approval needed from AniBT; works immediately if your account can post on Nyaa.
- **[Platform Proxy](/en/docs/nyaa-proxy)**: AniBT's official account uploads to Nyaa on your behalf. Ideal for teams without a Nyaa account or facing regional network blocks. Requires whitelist approval.

## Setup Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Verify Nyaa Account Access
    Sign in to [nyaa.si](https://nyaa.si) in your browser and ensure the account has active posting privileges and is in good standing.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Enter Credentials in AniBT
    Navigate to your fansub console at `/groups/<slug>/sync`, find the **Nyaa Sync** card, toggle it on, enter your Nyaa username and password, and click **Save**.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Test Account Connection
    Click the **Test Account** button. AniBT will verify the login session. This test only checks authentication validity and does not upload any test torrents to Nyaa.
  </Step>
</Steps>
