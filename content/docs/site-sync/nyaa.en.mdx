---
title: Nyaa Sync
description: Fill in the account credential required by AniBT's Nyaa sync.
---

Nyaa Sync uses Nyaa's official `/api/upload` endpoint. After a subtitle group stores its own Nyaa username and password in AniBT, the background worker uses that account to sync releases to Nyaa.

<Callout type="warn">
  Credentials are encrypted at rest, but you should still use a dedicated Nyaa publishing account instead of a personal main account.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  You can also apply for the [Nyaa proxy allowlist](/en/docs/nyaa-proxy). Sync and proxy publishing are separate features: sync uses your group's own Nyaa account; proxy publishing requires admin allowlist approval.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Prepare a Nyaa account

    Confirm that the account can publish on Nyaa. A dedicated group publishing account is recommended.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Open sync settings

    In AniBT, open the group settings page and find **Nyaa Sync**.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Enter username and password

    Enter the Nyaa username and password. After saving, the page only shows a masked account suffix and never displays the plaintext password.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Test the account

    Click **Test account**. The background worker performs a Nyaa API authentication probe and does not upload a real release.
  </Step>
</Steps>
