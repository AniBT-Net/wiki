---
title: Nyaa Sync
description: Enter the Nyaa username and password.
---

Sync uses the group's own Nyaa account. This is not [Nyaa proxy publishing](/en/docs/nyaa-proxy): proxy publishing uses the platform account and requires allowlist access.

<Callout type="warn">
  Use a dedicated Nyaa publishing account. Do not reuse a personal main-account password.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Enter credentials and test

    Confirm the account can publish on Nyaa. Subtitle group sync settings → **Nyaa Sync**, enter username and password, then save. Select **Test account**; the test does not upload a real release.
  </Step>
</Steps>
