title: Bangumi Moe Sync

---

Configuring Bangumi Moe sync automatically cross-publishes new releases from AniBT to the Bangumi Moe platform.

<Callout type="warn">
  Bangumi Moe accounts must be **email activated**; unverified accounts cannot authenticate. Legacy browser cookie configurations are discontinued; enter your actual username or email alongside your password.
</Callout>

## Setup Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Bangumi Moe Sync Settings
    Navigate to your fansub console at `/groups/<slug>/sync`, find the **Bangumi Moe** card, toggle it on, and click **Edit Credentials**.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Enter Credentials & Publishing Identity
    Enter the username or email and password for Bangumi Moe. If publishing under a fansub Team, specify the Team Name or Team ID; leave blank if publishing under a personal account.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Save and Test Connection
    Click **Save**, then click **Test Connection**. This test only validates authentication and team permissions without uploading any torrents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Field Descriptions

<TypeTable
  type={{
    'Username or Email': {

    },
    'Team Name': {
      type: 'string',
      default: 'Optional',
      description: 'Name of the Bangumi Moe fansub Team. Leave blank to publish under personal profile.',
    },
    'Team ID': {
      type: 'string',

      description: 'Exact ID of the Bangumi Moe team; prioritized over name matching if both provided.',
    },
  }}
/>
