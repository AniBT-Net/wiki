---
title: Bangumi Moe Sync
description: Configure release sync with a Bangumi Moe username or email and password.
---

Bangumi Moe sync signs in with an account password. AniBT stores the credentials encrypted and reuses the derived login session. The background worker signs in again after the session expires.

<Callout type="warn">
  Activate the Bangumi Moe account by email before configuring sync. Pasted browser Cookies are no longer supported; groups that only had a Cookie configured must enter a username and password.
</Callout>

## Configure the Account

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Bangumi Moe sync settings

    Open `/groups/<slug>/sync` in the AniBT subtitle group dashboard and select **Bangumi Moe Sync**.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Enter the username and password

    Enable Bangumi Moe sync. Select **Change credentials**, then enter the Bangumi Moe username or email and password.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Configure the publishing identity

    To publish through a Bangumi Moe Team, enter the publishing identity name or Team ID. Leave both fields empty to publish as the account itself.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Save the configuration

    Select **Save**. Changing the username or password clears the previous session; AniBT signs in again during the next test or publish attempt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test the Connection

After saving the credentials, select **Test Connection**.

- **Connection successful** — the account is signed in and can access the Bangumi Moe publishing feature.
- **Account not activated** — activate the account by email on Bangumi Moe, then test again.
- **Login failed** — check the username or email and password.
- **Login too frequent** — wait before testing again instead of repeatedly signing in.
