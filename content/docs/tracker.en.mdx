AniBT operates a high-speed BitTorrent tracker specifically dedicated to anime releases published on our platform.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Whitelist Communication Policy**: To prevent traffic abuse and spam torrents, the AniBT Tracker enforces an infohash whitelist. Only torrents successfully published on AniBT are automatically registered into the whitelist. Unregistered torrents announcing to this tracker will receive a `not whitelisted` error.
</Callout>

## Official Tracker Announce Address

When creating `.torrent` files or configuring trackers in your BitTorrent client, use the standard announce URL:

```txt title="AniBT Tracker Announce URL"
https://tracker.anibt.net/announce
```

- **Protocol**: Standard HTTP / HTTPS (Port 443), fully dual-stack IPv4 and IPv6 enabled.
- **Disabled Endpoints**: To ensure high availability and prevent DDoS attacks, the `/scrape` endpoint, UDP protocol, and deprecated `/announce/v1/*` paths are permanently closed.

## Torrent Creation and Publishing Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Add Tracker in Torrent Creator
    When creating a torrent with qBittorrent, Transmission, or mktorrent, place `https://tracker.anibt.net/announce` at the top of your tracker list.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Publish Release on AniBT
    Upload the generated `.torrent` file via your fansub group console or through the [Release API](/en/docs/open-api/releases-publish). Once accepted into the catalog, its infohash is instantly added to the tracker whitelist and included in the resulting magnet URI.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Seed from Your Client
    Load the torrent into your seeding client and verify that the tracker status shows `working`. Downloader clients fetching magnets or torrents from AniBT will immediately establish peer connections with you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

AniBT provides direct binary `.torrent` download endpoints for third-party downloaders and automation scripts:
