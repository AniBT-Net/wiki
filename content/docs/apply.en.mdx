---
title: Apply for admission
description: Email from the registered address to request subtitle-group publishing access.
---

Publishing access is not self-serve. Send from the **AniBT registered email** to [support@anibt.net](mailto:support@anibt.net), subject: `字幕组入驻申请 - <组名>`.

## Email contents

- **Group name**
- **slug**: a stable English identifier, e.g. `sakura-sub`. It appears in public URLs after approval and should not be changed.
- **Intro**: kinds of works, activity.
- **Contact**: a channel that can reach the subtitle group.
- **Identity proof (one of)**: an account on another BT site, admin proof on a public channel, or equivalent.

If you have not registered yet, finish [Account](/docs/account-auth) first.

Replies usually arrive within a few working days. If there is no reply after a week, check spam and send once more.

After approval, the slug is bound, an API Key is issued, and the group appears in the on-site list and RSS. Then configure [Site sync](/docs/site-sync), [Tracker](/docs/tracker), and the [Publish API](/docs/open-api/reference).
