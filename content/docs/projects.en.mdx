<GithubInfo owner="Yuri-NagaSaki" repo="FontInAss" />

Subtitle font subsetting. Scans characters actually used in ASS / SRT, cuts a subset, and ships it with the release. Site: [font.anibt.net](https://font.anibt.net).

1. Upload ASS / SRT and the original fonts.
2. Generate subsets (`.ttf` / `.otf`).
3. Put them in `Fonts/` and pack them into the torrent with the subtitles.

<GithubInfo owner="Yuri-NagaSaki" repo="AniBT-Speed" />

Downloads from RSS automatically and seeds long-term. Subscribe to [`/rss/anime.xml`](/docs/open-api/rss-anime) or [`/rss/magnets.xml`](/docs/open-api/other-endpoints).

Adjacent tools can be added with a PR to the [wiki repo](https://github.com/AniBT-Net/wiki).
